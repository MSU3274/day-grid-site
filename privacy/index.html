Day Grid Privacy Policy

Effective date: July 21, 2026

Day Grid is an offline-first productivity application developed by MSU Agency and published on Google Play by GlowNow. GlowNow manages the Google Play distribution account for Day Grid.

Information stored on your device

Day Grid stores tasks, categories, habits, habit completions, focus sessions, preferences, and settings locally on your device using private application storage.

Information we do not collect or share

Day Grid does not operate user accounts, servers, cloud synchronization, advertising, analytics, tracking, or remote configuration. Day Grid does not collect or share personal data with GlowNow, MSU Agency, advertisers, data brokers, or other third parties.

The application does not request access to your location, camera, microphone, contacts, calendar, health information, SMS messages, phone calls, Bluetooth devices, or advertising identifiers.

Backups and device transfer

If operating-system backup or device transfer is enabled, Android or iOS may include Day Grid's private application data in an encrypted platform-managed backup or device-to-device transfer. These features are controlled by you through your device or platform-account settings. GlowNow, MSU Agency, and Day Grid cannot access those platform-managed backups.

Manual backup files

Day Grid can create a JSON backup only when you request an export. Day Grid does not upload or transmit that file. Import reads only the backup file you select and validates it before changing local data. You control where exported files are stored and shared.

Data retention and deletion

Local information remains on your device until you delete it, use the in-app delete-all-data action, or uninstall the application. The delete-all-data action removes Day Grid's active database records, local preferences, onboarding state, and recoverable focus-timer state from the device.

Copies stored in a manual backup or an operating-system-managed backup must be deleted through the location or platform that manages that backup.

Security

Day Grid limits its data handling to private local application storage and user-initiated document access. Because Day Grid does not operate a backend service, your planning information is not transmitted to GlowNow or MSU Agency.

Children's privacy

Day Grid is a general productivity application and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children or other users.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated if Day Grid's features or legal obligations change. Any updated policy will be published on this page with a revised effective date.

Contact

For privacy or support questions, contact the Google Play publisher, GlowNow, at info@glownow-app.nl.
